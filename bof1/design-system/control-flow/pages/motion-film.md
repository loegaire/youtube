# Motion Film Override

This page overrides the generated master where the database recommendation conflicts with the film medium.

- Style: Swiss Modernism 2.0 + restrained dark glassmorphism.
- Grid: 12 columns, 8 px base unit, fixed masthead and conclusion bands.
- Typography: Helvetica Neue/Inter for narrative; IBM Plex Mono for machine values.
- Background: neutral charcoal `#0A0D0B`; never pure black and never a green wash.
- Primary text: warm white `#F1F3EE`; muted text `#9EA8A0`.
- Recurring accent: quiet green `#8CCB9A`.
- Semantic exceptions: amber `#D8BE73` for addresses; coral `#F0786E` for corruption; pale mint `#B3D8C2` for CPU action.
- Glass: white at 4–8% opacity, one-pixel border, 8 px radius, shallow shadow. Never stack multiple glass effects for decoration.
- Forbidden: pills as containers, glowing blobs, ambient particles, ornamental circles, large soft shadows, rainbow palettes, and centered card grids without editorial tension.
- Motion: animate one or two explanatory elements at a time; no continuous decorative motion.
