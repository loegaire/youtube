# Design Frame

Near-black flat terminal language. Mint marks valid data and observed state, amber marks
interpretation and arithmetic, coral marks unsafe transformations, and off-white carries
the instructional reading layer. Every real terminal, source, and disassembly surface is
loaded from `evidence/`; reconstructed examples are labelled in-frame.
