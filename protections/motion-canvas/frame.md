# Thinh flat terminal YouTube frame

Use solid near-black backgrounds and opaque flat UI surfaces. Mint marks safe or active
state, amber marks interpreters and instructions, coral marks danger and writes, warm white
carries primary explanation, and muted mint-gray carries secondary labels.

No gradients, glass, blur, glow haze, warped texture, or generic neon blue/purple.

Use terminal/editor/machine metaphors with large code, crisp 2px rules, asymmetric hierarchy,
and visible causal state. Captions are a large Nerd Font overlay with a translucent near-black
rail and mint prompt chevron.
